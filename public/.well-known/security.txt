# If you discover a vulnerability in Gray Zone Monitor, please report it
# to security@grayzonemonitor.com. We will acknowledge receipt within
# 48 hours and provide an initial assessment within 7 business days.
#
# We do not pursue legal action against security researchers who:
# - Make a good faith effort to avoid privacy violations
# - Avoid destruction of data or degradation of services
# - Do not exploit vulnerabilities beyond what is necessary to confirm them
# - Report findings to us before public disclosure
#
# We commit to coordinated disclosure (90-day timeline from report).
